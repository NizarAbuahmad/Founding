The invitation

You are not booking a workshop. You are joining a founding collection.

Afro Baobab was created from a simple belief: African culture isn't something to look at from a distance. It's something to hear, hold, taste and take part in.

Before the doors open in Al Quoz, a small number of people will make the first works that live inside the hub.

Their pieces go on the wall. Their names go on the labels.

Once the collection is complete, it will never be recreated.

Founding Creators are credited in the opening exhibition and invited back to see their work in place. Collect it afterwards, or leave it with the permanent Community Collection.
Why Afro Baobab

Not a workshop. A cultural destination you help build.

Dubai has no shortage of places to look at culture. Afro Baobab is a place to take part in it — an immersive African cultural hub and art gallery opening in Al Quoz.

Six spaces, each one asking something of you. You drum, carve, paint, write and eat your way into stories that have travelled continents. Nothing behind glass.

It is being built now. Which is why the people who make its first works matter.

The baobab gives us our name — a tree that holds water through drought, gathers people into its shade, and outlives everyone who plants it.

The founder

Why this exists.

Saran Sow Barry, Founder

Saran spent years watching African culture arrive in Dubai as a decoration — a pattern on a wall, a drum in the corner of an event, a theme for a evening. Never as the thing itself.

Afro Baobab is the answer to that. A permanent home in Al Quoz where African creative traditions are the centre of the programme rather than a guest slot, and where visitors take part rather than look on.

She's building it now. The Founding Creators are the people who fill it first.

Founder quote — Saran to supply and approve.

Before you join

Questions

I haven't painted since school. Is that a problem?

No. These sessions are built for people who think they can't as much as for people who paint every weekend. Facilitators walk you through every stage, and nobody is graded.

Can I bring my child?

Yes. Designed for ages 5 and up, with an adult present throughout. Confirm whether the accompanying adult books a place and creates their own piece.

Can I join both experiences?

Yes, and many people do. They're held on separate days, and you'd have two pieces in the opening collection rather than one.

When do I get my artwork back?

After the opening month. You'll be invited to the hub to collect it in person. If you'd rather it stayed, leave it with the Community Collection and it remains part of the hub permanently.
