Where culture becomes experience.

Before you book

Questions

Do I need artistic experience?

None at all. These sessions are built for people who think they can't paint as much as for people who paint every weekend. Facilitators guide you through every stage.

Can children participate?

Yes, from age 8. Children must be accompanied by an adult throughout. Confirm whether the accompanying adult books a place and creates their own piece.

What happens to my artwork, and can I keep it?

It's displayed in Afro Baobab's first exhibition during opening month, with your name beside it. Afterwards it's yours — you'll be invited back to collect it in person, or you may donate it to our Community Collection.

Can I book both experiences?

Yes. Booked together they're AED 500 instead of 560, and you'd have two pieces in the opening collection rather than one. They're held on separate days.
